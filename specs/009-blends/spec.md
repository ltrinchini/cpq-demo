# 009 — Blends (v1.1)

## Goal

Allow a coffee made of several origins, each with a percentage.

## Scope

- The percentages add up to 100%.
- The green coffee price is the average of the origins' prices, weighted by their percentages.
- Impacts: pricing engine, configurator, frozen quote copy, PDF.

## Questions to settle before starting

- Maximum number of origins in a blend.
- Are blends saved as products, or entered for each configuration?

## Dependencies

v1 complete.
