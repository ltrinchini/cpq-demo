# 009 — Blends (v1.1)

## Goal

Allow a coffee made of several origins, each with a percentage.

## Scope

- Up to 3 origins per blend, percentages add up to 100%.
- The green coffee price is the average of the origins' prices, weighted by their percentages.
- Entered directly in the configurator for each configuration, not saved as a reusable product.
- Impacts: pricing engine, configurator, frozen quote copy, PDF.

## Out of scope (moved to v2)

- Saving a blend as a reusable product — see `014 — Saved blends`.

## Dependencies

v1 complete.
