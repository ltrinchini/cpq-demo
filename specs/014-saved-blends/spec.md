# 014 — Saved blends (v2)

## Goal

Let a visitor save a blend as a reusable product instead of re-entering
origins and percentages on every configuration.

## Scope

- A saved blend has a name, a list of origins and their percentages (same
  rules as `009 — Blends`: up to 3 origins, percentages add up to 100%).
- Pick a saved blend in the configurator, or still enter one-off
  percentages.
- Manage saved blends: create, edit, delete.

## Dependencies

`009 — Blends` complete.
